# MMFARM-POL-2025 License  
### Global Data Rights & Real-World Proof Protection Policy  
**Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™**

---

## 1. Ownership of Real-World Data  
All real-world data, metadata, proofs, logs, images, timestamps, and Data Units  
produced under the DGCP™ (Data Governance & Continuous Proof) system are the  
exclusive property of the human worker who generated them.

---

## 2. Restrictions on Use  
No individual, platform, AI system, enterprise, or government may:

- train AI models,  
- build datasets,  
- create synthetic derivatives,  
- replicate, redistribute, or resell DGCP™ data,  

without **explicit written licensing** from the data owner.

Unauthorized use is strictly prohibited.

---

## 3. AI Training & Licensing  
Any AI system using DGCP™ data (directly or indirectly) must:

- obtain a valid DGCP™ license,  
- compensate the data owner,  
- disclose all intended use cases.  

No entity may use DGCP™ data for AI model development without approval.

---

## 4. Tamper Prevention  
DGCP™ data may not be:

- altered,  
- corrupted,  
- time-shifted,  
- modified in any form.  

All timestamps, provenance records, and distributed proofs are legally protected  
and must remain immutable.

---

## 5. Transparency Requirement  
Any entity accessing DGCP™ data must disclose:

- purpose of data use,  
- duration of access,  
- method of processing,  
- economic benefit derived from the data.

---

## 6. Enforcement & Legal Standing  
Violations of MMFARM-POL-2025 may result in:

- global takedown requests,  
- legal enforcement,  
- financial penalties,  
- permanent revocation of access rights.

---

## 7. Permission to Use  
Licensing is valid only through:

- explicit written approval,  
- verifiable digital signature,  
- a formal DGCP™ licensing contract.

Any implied or assumed rights are invalid.

---

## 8. No Implied Rights  
Unless explicitly granted in writing by the data owner or MaMeeFarm™,  
**no rights are given or implied** under this license.

---

## 9. Global Applicability  
This license applies worldwide, regardless of:

- jurisdiction,  
- platform,  
- AI system,  
- distributed storage architecture.

DGCP™ protections follow the data wherever it exists.
