# DGCP™ — Data Rights & Worker-Owned Data Framework  
MMFARM-POL-2025

## Overview
DGCP™ (Data Governance & Continuous Proof) introduces a global architecture where
real-world data is owned by the worker who produces it. This repository defines
the Data Rights foundation that ensures AI systems, governments, and enterprises
must recognize, verify, and license human-generated truth.

DGCP™ establishes a provable, timestamped truth layer that synthetic data cannot
replace. Each unit of labor becomes a verifiable Data Unit with ownership,
licensing rules, and immutable provenance.

---

## Why Data Rights Matter
Modern platforms extract human data for free.  
AI models are trained on human labor without attribution, consent, or
compensation.

DGCP™ corrects this imbalance through one principle:

> **If AI benefits from human labor, it must license the data from its owner.**

This framework protects humanity from synthetic data dominance and restores
economic value to real-world work.

---

## Core Concepts

### **1. Data Unit (DU)**
A standardized, verifiable unit of real-world data that includes:
- Timestamp  
- Metadata  
- Environmental context  
- Proof-of-origin  
- Licensing rules  
- Distributed verification  

### **2. Worker Data Ownership**
The worker performing the labor is the sole owner of the data produced.  
Platforms, governments, and AI systems must respect this ownership.

### **3. Truth-Provenance Chain**
All DGCP™ data must be:
- traceable  
- immutable  
- tamper-resistant  
- tied to real labor  
- verified across distributed systems  

Synthetic or altered data is rejected.

---

## Architecture Layers
1. **Origin Layer** — physical labor  
2. **Proof Layer** — images, logs, metadata, timestamps  
3. **Verification Layer** — continuous validation  
4. **Rights Layer** — ownership & licensing  
5. **AI Integration Layer** — ethical and paid data use  

---

## Global Impact
DGCP™ solves three global crises:
1. Synthetic data overflow  
2. Invisible labor value  
3. AI training without rights or attribution  

The system returns control, value, and ownership to the worker.
