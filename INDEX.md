## Purpose
This index provides a structured overview of the DGCP™ Data Rights repository.
It guides governments, enterprises, AI developers, and investors through the
legal, technical, and economic architecture of worker-owned real-world data.

DGCP™ establishes a global standard where human truth is protected,
verifiable, and licensable.
