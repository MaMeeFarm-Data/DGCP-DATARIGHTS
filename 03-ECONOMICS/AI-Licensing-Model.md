# DGCP™ AI Licensing Model  
MMFARM-POL-2025  
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose  
This document defines the AI Licensing Model under DGCP™, establishing how
artificial intelligence systems must legally, ethically, and economically
license real-world human-generated data.

DGCP™ recognizes that AI value is derived from human labor and truth.
Therefore, AI usage of real-world data requires compensation to the data owner.

---

## 2. Core Licensing Principle  

> **If an AI system learns from human-generated data, it must pay the human who created it.**

DGCP™ rejects the assumption that public availability equals free usage.
Truth has ownership. Labor has value.

---

## 3. What Requires Licensing  
AI licensing is required when data is used for:

- model training  
- fine-tuning  
- reinforcement learning  
- dataset construction  
- behavior modeling  
- decision-making systems  
- predictive analytics  
- derivative synthetic data generation  

Any AI benefit derived from DGCP™ Data Units is subject to licensing.

---

## 4. Licensing Units  
Licensing is calculated based on:

### 4.1 Data Unit (DU)  
Each verified DGCP™ Data Unit represents a licensable economic unit.

### 4.2 Time-Based Usage  
Licensing duration is tied to:
- training cycles  
- inference lifespan  
- model retraining events  

### 4.3 Scope of Use  
Pricing adjusts based on:
- commercial vs non-commercial use  
- scale of deployment  
- geographic reach  
- revenue impact  

---

## 5. Licensing Models  

### Model A — Per-Data-Unit License  
AI systems pay per verified Data Unit consumed.

### Model B — Subscription License  
Recurring payment for continuous access to licensed data streams.

### Model C — Revenue-Sharing License  
Percentage-based compensation tied to AI-generated revenue.

### Model D — Sovereign / National License  
Governments license DGCP™ data on behalf of citizens.

Multiple models may coexist.

---

## 6. Worker Compensation Flow  
Licensing payments are distributed as follows:

1. Worker (primary owner)  
2. Verification & infrastructure maintenance  
3. DGCP™ governance reserve  

Workers always receive the majority share.

---

## 7. AI Compliance Requirements  
AI systems must:

- declare training intent  
- disclose data sources  
- log Data Unit consumption  
- respect worker revocation rights  
- expose audit trails  
- relicense upon retraining  

Failure to comply constitutes data theft.

---

## 8. Prohibited AI Practices  
The following actions are forbidden:

- training on unlicensed DGCP™ data  
- laundering data through synthetic datasets  
- obscuring provenance links  
- claiming fair use without consent  
- reselling derived models without licensing  

Violations trigger enforcement under MMFARM-POL-2025.

---

## 9. Global Economic Impact  
DGCP™ enables:
- fair compensation for invisible labor  
- ethical AI development  
- reduction of synthetic data dependence  
- new national data economies  
- global trust in AI systems  

It transforms data from extraction into partnership.

---

## 10. Conclusion  
The DGCP™ AI Licensing Model establishes a future where:
- humans own their data,  
- AI pays for truth,  
- and global intelligence grows without exploitation.

AI does not replace human labor —  
it licenses it.
