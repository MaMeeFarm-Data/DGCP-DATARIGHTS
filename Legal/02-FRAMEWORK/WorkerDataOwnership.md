# DGCP™ Worker Data Ownership Law  
MMFARM-POL-2025  
Version 1.0 — Global Draft

## 1. Legal Principle  
Under DGCP™, all real-world data generated by a worker is legally recognized as:  
**property, labor output, and an economic asset owned exclusively by the worker.**

This ownership extends to:
- raw data  
- metadata  
- timestamps  
- images, logs, videos  
- environmental readings  
- derivative data processed by AI  

No platform or institution may claim data ownership without explicit consent.

---

## 2. Scope of Protection  
Worker data ownership applies to any activity where:
- physical labor is performed,  
- real-world observations are recorded,  
- environmental or behavioral proof is captured,  
- or human-generated truth forms part of a Data Unit.

This includes agricultural work, manufacturing, caregiving, logistics, digital labor, and all forms of measurable human activity.

---

## 3. Exclusive Rights of the Worker  
Each worker holds the following enforceable rights:

### 3.1 Right of Possession  
The worker possesses full ownership and control of the data produced during labor.

### 3.2 Right of Use  
The worker may use, store, or distribute their data as they choose.

### 3.3 Right of Licensing  
Any AI system, enterprise, or government must obtain paid licensing before using DGCP™ data.

### 3.4 Right of Revocation  
The worker may withdraw access or licensing at any time unless bound by contract.

### 3.5 Right of Attribution  
Any use of the data must preserve the worker’s identity or anonymization preference.

---

## 4. Prohibited Actions (Without Consent)  
No entity may:
- harvest or monitor labor data,  
- scrape DGCP™ metadata,  
- use or sell worker-generated data,  
- train AI models using DGCP™ datasets,  
- store DGCP™ data outside licensed boundaries.

Violations fall under global enforcement of MMFARM-POL-2025.

---

## 5. Enforcement & Penalties  
Entities violating worker data rights are subject to:
- data takedown orders,  
- revocation of access,  
- financial damages,  
- legal prosecution in the worker’s jurisdiction,  
- global notification of non-compliance.

DGCP™ maintains immutable logs as evidence.

---

## 6. Worker Consent Standard  
Consent must be:
- explicit,  
- verifiable,  
- timestamped,  
- contractually recorded.

Implicit or assumed consent is invalid.

---

## 7. Integration With Global AI Standards  
AI systems must:
1. declare training intent,  
2. disclose data usage,  
3. provide economic compensation,  
4. maintain provenance links to DGCP™ Data Units,  
5. uphold worker data rights at all stages of model development.

---

## 8. Conclusion  
This legal file establishes the world’s first enforceable definition of **Worker Data Ownership** in the era of AI.  
It ensures that human labor shapes the global economy—not anonymously, but with rights, value, and protection.

DGCP™ transforms workers into recognized data producers  
and AI into systems that must respect and compensate human truth.
