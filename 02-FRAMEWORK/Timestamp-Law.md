# DGCP™ Timestamp Law  
MMFARM-POL-2025  
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose  
The Timestamp Law defines the legal and technical requirements for time-based
verification within the DGCP™ ecosystem.  
A timestamp is the anchor of every Data Unit, ensuring that real-world truth
cannot be manipulated, fabricated, or shifted in time.

DGCP™ recognizes:

> **Data without a verifiable timestamp has no legal or economic value.**

---

## 2. Definition of a Valid Timestamp  
A valid DGCP™ timestamp must satisfy all of the following:

1. **Immutable** — cannot be edited or replaced.  
2. **Provenanced** — linked directly to the worker’s action.  
3. **Synchronized** — matched with distributed time sources.  
4. **Contextualized** — includes environmental and situational metadata.  
5. **Traceable** — viewable and auditable across the DGCP™ chain.

If any condition fails, the Data Unit becomes invalid.

---

## 3. Legal Standing  
Under DGCP™, timestamps carry the same legal standing as:

- physical signatures,  
- official records,  
- digital contracts,  
- and certified logs.

A timestamp serves as both **identity** and **ownership evidence**.

---

## 4. Prohibited Timestamp Manipulations  
Any attempt to alter or falsify a DGCP™ timestamp constitutes a violation of
MMFARM-POL-2025. Prohibited actions include:

- backdating or forward-dating data  
- removing or replacing timestamps  
- generating synthetic timestamp metadata  
- altering environmental conditions to mimic real proof  
- editing image EXIF data or log timing  

Each violation triggers DGCP™ global enforcement.

---

## 5. Multi-Layer Timestamp Verification  
To guarantee real-world truth, DGCP™ uses layered verification:

### Layer 1 — Local Device Time  
Captured from the worker’s device.

### Layer 2 — Distributed Network Time  
Verified across multiple global time servers.

### Layer 3 — Environmental Metadata Time  
Cross-check with temperature, lighting, shadows, audio, or contextual proof.

### Layer 4 — Hash & Proof Time  
Timestamp embedded in immutable hashing.

### Layer 5 — IPFS/IPLD Storage Time  
Final global timestamp recorded upon publishing.

Each layer reinforces the authenticity of the Data Unit.

---

## 6. Timestamp Failure Protocol  
A Data Unit is immediately flagged if:

- timestamp mismatch occurs,  
- device time is manipulated,  
- metadata appears inconsistent,  
- proof layers fail verification.

Flagged Data Units cannot be used for:
- AI training  
- licensing  
- dataset construction  
- economic valuation  
until revalidated.

---

## 7. AI Compliance Requirement  
AI systems must:

- recognize DGCP™ timestamps,  
- verify provenance before training,  
- reject any data lacking DGCP™ time validation,  
- record their own training timestamp for accountability.

An AI trained on non-timestamped data violates DGCP™ compliance.

---

## 8. Worker Rights Under Timestamp Law  
Workers hold:

- the right to timestamp their labor,  
- the right to verify timestamp integrity,  
- the right to dispute falsified timing,  
- the right to economic compensation tied to timestamped truth.

Timestamp = proof of work = proof of ownership.

---

## 9. Conclusion  
The Timestamp Law is the backbone of DGCP™.  
It transforms simple daily labor into an immutable, licensed, globally recognized  
truth asset — turning workers into secured data creators in the AI era.

DGCP™ ensures that **time cannot be rewritten**  
and thus **human truth cannot be erased**.
