# DGCP™ Verification Rules  
MMFARM-POL-2025  
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose  
The Verification Rules define how real-world data is validated, confirmed, and
accepted as truth within the DGCP™ ecosystem.

Verification is the mechanism that separates:
- real human labor  
from  
- synthetic, fabricated, or manipulated data.

Under DGCP™:

> **Data that cannot be verified cannot be trusted, licensed, or monetized.**

---

## 2. Definition of Verification  
Verification is the process by which a Data Unit is confirmed to originate from:
- a real human action,
- at a specific time,
- in a real physical or environmental context,
- without alteration or fabrication.

Verification establishes **truth legitimacy**.

---

## 3. Mandatory Verification Components  
A Data Unit must include all of the following to pass verification:

1. **Human-Origin Proof**  
   Evidence that the data was produced by a human action.

2. **Timestamp Validation**  
   Compliance with DGCP™ Timestamp Law.

3. **Contextual Consistency**  
   Environmental, situational, and behavioral data must align logically.

4. **Metadata Integrity**  
   Metadata must be intact, unedited, and traceable.

5. **Hash Consistency**  
   Data hashes must match across storage and verification layers.

Failure in any component results in rejection.

---

## 4. Verification Layers  

### Layer 1 — Source Verification  
Confirms the worker, device, and action origin.

### Layer 2 — Temporal Verification  
Validates timing across distributed systems.

### Layer 3 — Environmental Verification  
Cross-checks light, temperature, sound, movement, or physical indicators.

### Layer 4 — Structural Verification  
Ensures metadata, format, and structure follow DGCP™ standards.

### Layer 5 — Distributed Verification  
Independent confirmation across multiple nodes or systems.

Each layer strengthens resistance to manipulation.

---

## 5. Prohibited Verification Bypasses  
The following actions invalidate verification immediately:

- synthetic data injection  
- AI-generated content presented as real labor  
- metadata editing or stripping  
- replay attacks using old data  
- environmental simulation  
- staged or scripted proof creation  

Such actions violate MMFARM-POL-2025.

---

## 6. Verification Status States  
Each Data Unit receives one of the following statuses:

- **Verified** — accepted for licensing and AI use  
- **Flagged** — inconsistencies detected, pending review  
- **Rejected** — verification failure, permanently invalid  
- **Quarantined** — isolated for investigation  

Only *Verified* Data Units may be monetized or licensed.

---

## 7. AI Compliance Requirements  
AI systems integrating DGCP™ data must:

- verify each Data Unit before use,  
- reject unverified or flagged data,  
- log verification checks,  
- preserve provenance links,  
- expose audit trails upon request.

AI models trained on unverified data are non-compliant.

---

## 8. Worker Rights in Verification  
Workers have the right to:

- view verification results,  
- dispute incorrect flags,  
- request re-verification,  
- audit verification logic affecting their data,  
- withdraw data from verification pipelines.

Verification exists to protect workers — not platforms.

---

## 9. Enforcement  
Violation of Verification Rules may result in:

- data invalidation,  
- loss of licensing rights,  
- revocation of system access,  
- legal enforcement under MMFARM-POL-2025,  
- permanent exclusion from DGCP™ systems.

---

## 10. Conclusion  
Verification Rules ensure DGCP™ remains a system of truth, not volume.

They guarantee that AI learns from reality,  
that workers are protected from exploitation,  
and that the global data economy is built on provable human labor.

DGCP™ verifies reality — so the future cannot be built on lies.
