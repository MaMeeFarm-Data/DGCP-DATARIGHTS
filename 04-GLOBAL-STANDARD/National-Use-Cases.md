# DGCP™ National Use Cases
MMFARM-POL-2025
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose
This document presents real-world national use cases for deploying DGCP™
as a trusted data rights and AI governance infrastructure.

Each use case demonstrates how DGCP™ converts human labor into
verifiable, owned, and licensable data while protecting citizens and
enabling ethical AI adoption.

---

## 2. Agriculture & Food Security

### Problem
Farmers generate critical data (yield, soil, weather, labor)
but do not own or monetize it. AI systems extract this data
without compensation.

### DGCP™ Solution
- Each farming activity becomes a Data Unit.
- Farmers retain ownership of production data.
- AI models license verified agricultural data.
- Governments gain trusted food security intelligence.

### Outcome
- Fair farmer compensation
- Reduced data exploitation
- Trusted AI-driven food planning

---

## 3. Labor & Workforce Data

### Problem
Workers produce valuable behavioral and productivity data
that platforms monetize without transparency.

### DGCP™ Solution
- Work activities generate worker-owned Data Units.
- Licensing required for workforce analytics or AI training.
- Labor data recognized as economic output.

### Outcome
- Data-as-labor economy
- Fair compensation models
- Reduced worker surveillance abuse

---

## 4. Public Health & Caregiving

### Problem
Caregivers and health workers generate real-world data
that is often unverified, fragmented, or exploited.

### DGCP™ Solution
- Care activities timestamped and verified.
- Data Units used for AI health research under license.
- Privacy preserved while truth remains auditable.

### Outcome
- Trusted public health AI
- Ethical medical data usage
- Stronger caregiver protections

## 5. Smart Cities & Infrastructure

### Problem
Urban data is collected at scale but lacks provenance,
leading to mistrust and manipulation.

### DGCP™ Solution
- City sensors and workers produce verifiable Data Units.
- Provenance ensures data integrity.
- AI systems operate under compliance rules.

### Outcome
- Trusted smart city AI
- Reduced misinformation
- Transparent urban governance

## 6. Education & Skill Development

### Problem
Learning data is extracted by platforms without benefit
to learners or educators.

### DGCP™ Solution
- Learning activities generate Data Units.
- AI training requires licensing.
- Skills data owned by individuals.

### Outcome
- Learner-owned digital credentials
- Ethical AI education tools
- Portable skill provenance
