# DGCP™ AI Compliance & Audit Framework
MMFARM-POL-2025
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose
This framework defines mandatory compliance requirements for any AI system
that accesses, processes, trains on, or derives value from DGCP™ Data Units.
It ensures enforceability, auditability, and worker compensation across the
entire AI lifecycle.

---

## 2. Scope
Applies to:
- foundation models
- fine-tuned models
- agents and inference services
- data pipelines and labeling systems
- derivative and synthetic data generators

Any benefit from DGCP™ data triggers compliance.

---

## 3. Pre-Use Requirements (Before Training / Inference)
AI systems must:
1. Declare intent and scope of use.
2. Identify specific DGCP™ Data Units.
3. Verify provenance and timestamps.
4. Obtain valid licensing.
5. Register compliance metadata.

Unregistered use is prohibited.

---

## 4. In-Use Requirements (During Operation)
AI systems must:
- enforce access controls per license,
- log Data Unit consumption,
- preserve provenance links,
- prevent data mixing with unverified sources,
- support real-time audit hooks.

Any deviation flags non-compliance.

---

## 5. Post-Use Requirements (After Training / Deployment)
AI systems must:
- retain audit logs,
- disclose retraining events,
- re-license upon model updates,
- support revocation impacts,
- provide compliance reports upon request.

Derived models remain bound by original licenses.

---

## 6. Auditability
DGCP™ supports:
- scheduled audits,
- random audits,
- incident-triggered audits,
- third-party audits.

Audit artifacts include hashes, timestamps, logs, and license proofs.

---

## 7. Prohibited Practices
AI systems may not:
- train on unlicensed DGCP™ data,
- obscure or break provenance,
- launder data via synthetic layers,
- claim fair use without consent,
- deny audit access.

Violations trigger enforcement.

---

## 8. Enforcement Actions
Non-compliance may result in:
- immediate access revocation,
- model de-listing,
- licensing termination,
- financial penalties,
- public non-compliance notices,
- legal action under MMFARM-POL-2025.

---

## 9. Worker Rights
Workers retain:
- right to attribution or anonymity,
- right to compensation,
- right to revoke future use,
- right to audit usage of their data.

Compliance exists to protect workers first.

---

## 10. Conclusion
DGCP™ AI Compliance transforms ethics into enforcement.
AI may innovate freely — but only by respecting
human-generated truth, ownership, and rights.
