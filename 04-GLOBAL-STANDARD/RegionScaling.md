# DGCP™ Regional & Global Scaling Framework  
MMFARM-POL-2025  
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose
This document defines how DGCP™ scales from a single worker or farm
to regional, national, and global adoption without compromising
data rights, verification integrity, or worker ownership.

DGCP™ is designed to scale **horizontally**, not extractively.

---

## 2. Core Scaling Principle
> **Scale the system, not the exploitation.**

DGCP™ expands by adding verified participants, not by centralizing control.
Ownership remains local. Verification becomes global.

---

## 3. Tiered Scaling Architecture

### Tier 1 — Local (Individual / Farm / Community)
- Single worker or small group
- Direct data capture
- Manual or semi-automated verification
- Local economic impact

### Tier 2 — Regional (Province / State)
- Shared verification infrastructure
- Regional timestamp synchronization
- Local governance nodes
- Aggregated but owner-controlled data pools

### Tier 3 — National
- National DGCP™ registry
- Government-recognized Data Units
- AI licensing at national scale
- Citizen-level data rights enforcement

### Tier 4 — Global
- Cross-border provenance validation
- Interoperable licensing
- International AI compliance
- Global auditability

---

## 4. Governance Model
Each region operates under:
- local worker ownership
- regional verification councils
- national compliance standards
- global DGCP™ interoperability rules

No central authority owns the data.
Governance coordinates — it does not control.

---

## 5. Economic Scaling
As regions scale:
- Data Units aggregate in value, not ownership
- Workers receive proportional compensation
- Licensing scales by volume and scope
- Infrastructure costs decrease per unit

DGCP™ enables fair economies of scale.

---

## 6. Interoperability Standards
Regional systems must support:
- DGCP™ Timestamp Law
- Verification Rules
- Provenance Framework
- MMFARM-POL-2025 License
- Open audit interfaces

Non-compliant regions cannot federate.

---

## 7. Risk Mitigation
DGCP™ scaling prevents:
- data monopolization
- synthetic data laundering
- cross-border data theft
- centralized control failure

Each region remains resilient and autonomous.

---

## 8. Adoption Path
1. Pilot community deployment
2. Regional validation
3. National integration
4. Global federation

Scaling is deliberate, verifiable, and irreversible.

---

## 9. Conclusion
DGCP™ proves that a system can scale globally
without erasing human ownership.

From one worker to the world,
DGCP™ keeps truth local — and trust global.
