# DGCP™ Dispute Resolution & Enforcement Framework
MMFARM-POL-2025
Version 1.0 — © 2025 MaMeeFarm™

## 1. Purpose
This document defines how disputes related to DGCP™ Data Units are reported,
reviewed, adjudicated, and resolved. It provides a structured, transparent,
and enforceable process to protect worker rights, data integrity, and lawful AI use.

DGCP™ resolves disputes through evidence, not power.

---

## 2. Scope of Disputes
This framework applies to disputes involving:
- data ownership and attribution,
- licensing and compensation,
- provenance and timestamp integrity,
- verification outcomes,
- AI compliance violations,
- unauthorized data use or extraction,
- revocation and access termination.

---

## 3. Evidence Standard
All disputes are evaluated based on DGCP™ evidence, including:
- immutable timestamps,
- provenance chains,
- verification logs,
- cryptographic hashes,
- licensing records,
- audit trails.

Unverified or synthetic evidence is inadmissible.

---

## 4. Dispute Initiation
A dispute may be initiated by:
- the worker (data owner),
- a licensed AI or enterprise,
- a verifier node,
- a government authority,
- DGCP™ governance body.

Each case must include a timestamped claim and supporting evidence.

---

## 5. Resolution Process

### Step 1 — Intake & Validation
- Claim is logged and timestamped.
- Evidence is checked for completeness.
- Parties are notified.

### Step 2 — Preliminary Review
- Verification of provenance and licensing.
- Temporary measures may be applied (e.g., access suspension).

### Step 3 — Adjudication
- Review by DGCP™ Arbitration Panel.
- Evidence-based determination.
- No anonymous or undocumented claims.

### Step 4 — Decision & Enforcement
- Binding resolution issued.
- Enforcement actions applied if necessary.
- Decision recorded immutably.

---

## 6. Arbitration Panel
Panels may include:
- DGCP™ legal representatives,
- independent verifiers,
- domain experts,
- government observers (where applicable).

Panels operate transparently and without conflict of interest.

---

## 7. Remedies & Enforcement
Possible remedies include:
- compensation payment,
- license termination,
- access revocation,
- data takedown,
- public non-compliance notice,
- legal escalation under MMFARM-POL-2025.

Remedies are proportional to harm and intent.

---

## 8. Appeals
Parties may appeal decisions if:
- new verified evidence emerges,
- procedural errors are identified.

All appeals are timestamped and logged.
Final decisions remain auditable.

---

## 9. Cross-Border Disputes
DGCP™ supports:
- multi-jurisdiction arbitration,
- mutual recognition of evidence,
- treaty-based enforcement.

Provenance transcends borders.

---

## 10. Conclusion
The DGCP™ Dispute Resolution Framework ensures that
conflicts are resolved through truth, evidence, and fairness.

In the DGCP™ system:
**proof replaces power, and data justice protects humanity.**
